# Bilingual Language Pack Manual Test Checklist

1. Open Blender 5.0.1 portable.
2. Enable Quick Language Switcher.
3. Open add-on preferences through `Manage Languages...`.
4. Read the Bilingual Language Packs warning.
5. Click `Install Bilingual Language Pack`.
6. Confirm the report says restart is required.
7. Restart Blender.
8. Open Preferences > Interface > Translation > Language.
9. Confirm `zh_en` and `en_zh` appear.
10. Switch to `zh_en` and verify UI labels show Chinese plus English.
11. Switch to `en_zh` and verify UI labels show English plus Chinese.
12. Open Quick Language Switcher preferences.
13. Click `Uninstall Bilingual Language Pack`.
14. Restart Blender.
15. Confirm `zh_en` and `en_zh` no longer appear.
